# tx-eats

Three things built from one pipeline (modeled on `../co-eats/` and `../fl-eats/`, which are never modified):

1. **The web leaderboard**: every Texas restaurant on several boards, published as a private claude.ai Artifact from `site/` (generated by
   `scripts/make-leaderboard.py`). It uses the UCSD Google Local 2021 snapshot for ratings, labeled "2021" everywhere, so `site/` never goes
   in a public repo.
2. **Texas Eats**, a free iPhone and iPad app (`TexasEats/`, XcodeGen `project.yml`). No Google-derived data: places come from TABC
   licenses, three cities' inspection records and Overture Maps open data. Ratings and hours come live from Apple Maps' own place card.
3. **The website** in `docs/` (generated by `scripts/make-site.py`): landing page, guides, city pages, the web app at `/explore/`,
   privacy, terms. QA with `scripts/qa-site.py`.

See `CLAUDE.md` for the rules and commands, and `playbook/12-sources.md` for sources and calibration.

## Official records
- **TABC License Information** (data.texas.gov) and the **Comptroller's Mixed Beverage Gross Receipts**: reported alcohol sales, shown
  as reported.
- **Inspection scores** as Austin, El Paso and Arlington publish them (score out of 100 and each city's own band or grade). Texas has no
  statewide grade, and neither do we.

## Hand-checked research
`data/research/` (private) holds the MICHELIN Guide Texas selection, James Beard honors (finalists never called wins), and regional lists of
barbecue joints, Tex-Mex, taquerías, kolaches, chicken-fried steak and the oldest places, each with a 2025–26 source.
