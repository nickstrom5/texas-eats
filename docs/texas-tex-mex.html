The combination plate

Tex-Mex is the Texas cooking that grew from Tejano kitchens: yellow cheese enchiladas under chili con carne gravy, crispy tacos, rice and refried beans on one hot plate, chips with salsa and a bowl of queso. Fajitas came out of the South Texas ranch country, and San Antonio claims the puffy taco.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Houston (10), San Antonio (9), Austin (3), Fort Worth (3), Dallas (3), El Paso (2). The dishes we found most often: enchiladas (6), fajitas (4), nachos (3), queso (3), cheese enchiladas (3), puffy tacos (3).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Barbecue joints sell out and keep short hours, so check before you drive.
