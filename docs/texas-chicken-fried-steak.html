The state plate

Chicken-fried steak is round steak pounded thin, dredged in seasoned flour, fried crisp like fried chicken and smothered in peppery cream gravy, usually with mashed potatoes and Texas toast. Small-town cafés and old steakhouses make it best, and many serve it at lunch only.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Pearland (8), Fort Worth (5), San Antonio (2), Marble Falls (1), Austin (1), Houston (1), Strawn (1). The dishes we found most often: chicken-fried steak (8).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Barbecue joints sell out and keep short hours, so check before you drive.
