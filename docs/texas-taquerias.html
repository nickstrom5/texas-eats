Tortillas first

A Texas taquería runs on its tortillas: corn for al pastor off the trompo, carnitas, barbacoa and guisados, flour for the breakfast tacos of egg, potato, bacon and chorizo that Austin and San Antonio argue over. Many are counters, trucks and drive-throughs open early in the morning.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Houston (25), Austin (6), Fort Worth (6), Dallas (5), El Paso (3), Corpus Christi (2), Mission (1), Arlington (1). The dishes we found most often: breakfast tacos (8), barbacoa taco (5), barbacoa (4), al pastor taco (4), trompo taco (4), birria (3).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Barbecue joints sell out and keep short hours, so check before you drive.
