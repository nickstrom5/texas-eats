Smoke, salt and pepper

Central Texas barbecue is beef brisket rubbed with salt and black pepper and smoked for hours over post oak, sold by the pound on butcher paper with white bread, pickles and onions. Beef ribs, pork ribs, turkey and house-made sausage share the cutting board. East Texas leans to chopped beef and sauce; South Texas has barbacoa on Sunday mornings. Many joints open a few days a week and close when the meat runs out.

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Barbecue joints sell out and keep short hours, so check before you drive.
