Austin restaurants: MICHELIN & James Beard, barbecue, taquerías and more

What Austin has on our lists: 46 MICHELIN or James Beard honorees, 13 hand-checked barbecue joints, 6 taquerías, 6 kolache bakeries, 3 Tex-Mex restaurants, 2 of Texas's oldest restaurants (back to 1866) and 1 chicken-fried steak spot. Each hand-checked place had a 2025 or 2026 source when we looked in fall 2026. In all, Texas Eats maps 4,050 restaurants, cafés, bars and bakeries in Austin, nearest first on your phone.

Oldest restaurants in Austin

In business at this address since 1960 or before, going by the place itself or a published history, listed from the oldest. Each note gives the source of the year. Across Texas: Texas's oldest restaurants.

  1. Scholz Garten

    1607 San Jacinto Blvd, Austin

    On the menu: bratwurst, schnitzel · Since 1866

    Beer garden and restaurant near the Capitol, established by German immigrant August Scholz in 1866 and serving brats, schnitzel and beer.

    Wikipedia: Scholz bought the lot at 1607 San Jacinto Boulevard in 1862 and established the garten there in 1866 (it notes a closure during Prohibition); Tasting Table (2025) gives the same address.

  2. Dirty Martin's Place

    2808 Guadalupe St, Austin

    On the menu: chili cheeseburger, fried pickles, 1926 Nachos · Since 1926

    Guadalupe Street burger joint, "an Austin tradition since 1926," that began as a dirt-floored drive-in.

    Its own site: since 1926. The Austin Chronicle lists it at 2808 Guadalupe and says it began there as a drive-through with a dirt floor.
