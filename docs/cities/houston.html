Houston restaurants: MICHELIN & James Beard, taquerías, barbecue and more

What Houston has on our lists: 46 MICHELIN or James Beard honorees, 25 taquerías, 10 hand-checked barbecue joints, 10 Tex-Mex restaurants, 4 kolache bakeries, 1 chicken-fried steak spot and 1 of Texas's oldest restaurants (from 1923).
