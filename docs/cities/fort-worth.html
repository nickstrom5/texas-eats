Barbecue joints in Fort Worth

Each one checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source. For all of Texas: 74 Texas barbecue joints.

  1. Goldee's Bar-B-Q

    4645 Dick Price Rd, Fort Worth

    1 MICHELIN StarBarbecue On the menu: brisket, turkey, jalapeño-cheddar sausage, brisket beans, banana pudding

    Southeast Fort Worth joint with famously long lines, known for brisket and Lao-inspired sausage specials; holds a MICHELIN star.

  2. Panther City BBQ

    201 E Hattie St, Fort Worth

    MICHELIN RecommendedBarbecue On the menu: brisket, brisket elote, brisket burritos, pulled pork, spicy smoked mac and cheese

    Fort Worth barbecue on East Hattie Street, opened in 2019, known for brisket elote and pork belly poppers.

  3. Angelo's BBQ

    2533 White Settlement Rd, Fort Worth

    Barbecue On the menu: brisket, pork ribs, turkey, jalapeño sausage

    Old-school White Settlement Road barbecue house, opened in 1958, with brisket, ribs and jalapeño sausage.

  4. Brix Barbecue

    1012 S Main St, Fort Worth

    Barbecue On the menu: pork belly porchetta, tomahawk pork chop, steak sandwiches

    Near Southside barbecue that started as a trailer in 2017 and opened a brick-and-mortar in 2023.

  5. Heim Barbecue

    5333 White Settlement Rd, Fort Worth

    Barbecue On the menu: bacon burnt ends, brisket

    Fort Worth barbecue known for bacon burnt ends; its River District restaurant on White Settlement Road stayed open after the Magnolia flagship closed in February 2026.

  6. Smoke-a-Holics BBQ

    1417 Evans Ave, Fort Worth

    Barbecue On the menu: rib tips, oxtails, Cajun cream corn, Coca-Cola cake

    Southside "Tex-soul" barbecue with oxtails and rib tips.

Chicken-fried steak in Fort Worth

Each one checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source. For all of Texas: 19 Texas chicken-fried steak spots.

  1. Charleston's

    3020 S Hulen St, Fort Worth

    Chicken-fried steak On the menu: chicken-fried steak

    Hulen Street restaurant with an off-menu hand-breaded chicken-fried steak (ask for it), served with mashed potatoes and glazed carrots.

  2. Fred's Texas Cafe

    7101 Camp Bowie West Blvd, Fort Worth

    Chicken-fried steak On the menu: chicken-fried steak, Fred Burger, Bolo Burger

    Camp Bowie West burger joint serving since 1978; Fort Worth Weekly readers picked its chicken-fried steak in 2025 and 2026.

  3. Jesús Family Restaurant

    810 S Main St, Fort Worth

    Chicken-fried steak On the menu: chicken-fried steak, fried pie

    South Main family restaurant known for a light, crispy chicken-fried steak, house-made gravy and handmade fried pies.

  4. Rex's Bar & Grill

    1501 S University Dr, Fort Worth

    Chicken-fried steak On the menu: chicken-fried steak with jalapeño gravy, seafood gumbo

    University Drive sports bar from the owner of Ol' South Pancake House and former Reata chef Brian Olenjack, with chicken-fried steak under jalapeño gravy.

  5. West Side Café

    7950 Camp Bowie W Blvd, Fort Worth

    Chicken-fried steak On the menu: chicken-fried steak, homemade pies, breakfast burritos

    Camp Bowie West café opened in 1996, known for chicken-fried steak and homemade pies.
