Texas MICHELIN & James Beard restaurants

166 Texas restaurants recognized by MICHELIN or the James Beard Foundation. The MICHELIN Guide Texas selection accounts for 148 places (19 1-Star, 46 Bib Gourmand and 83 Recommended restaurants); 31 places hold a James Beard award or finalist nod from 2025 or 2026, or an America's Classics award.

What counts as an honor here

Two sources, both reported as facts rather than scores. One is the MICHELIN Guide Texas selection as MICHELIN lists it. The other is the James Beard Foundation: its 2025 and 2026 awards and finalists, plus America's Classics awards from any year (8 places). Finalists are labeled as exactly that, not as winners. A restaurant that has closed since it was honored is off the list, and where an honor went to a chef or a restaurant group instead of the restaurant, the line under the place says so. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count.

MICHELIN and the MICHELIN Guide are trademarks of Michelin. James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation. Texas Eats is not affiliated with either.
